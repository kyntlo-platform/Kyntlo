How templates are used

Templates are starting points, not finished products

When we set up your account, the Kyntlo team takes the templates that fit your business and adapts them to you. Nothing goes live with sample names, prices or placeholder text.

  1. Pick what fitsWe look at how you get customers today and choose the templates that match.
  2. We adapt them to youYour services, prices, opening hours, wording and branding go in. Sample data comes out.
  3. You check, then it goes liveYou review the pages and messages before anything is sent to a real customer.

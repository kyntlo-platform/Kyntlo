# Kyntlo Academy — Course Map

Status: Draft for owner review · Version 0.1 · 2026-10-21

Format for every video: 16:9 landscape, 1920×1080, 30 fps, warm female AI
narrator (Gemini TTS), burned-in subtitles + separate `.srt`, soft music bed
that ducks under the voice, Kyntlo-branded intro and outro.

Demo account: Kyntlo **Pro** plan (everything unlocked), filled with a
fictional business, **Bloom Dental Studio**. No real customer data appears on
screen. Each lesson is tagged with the lowest plan that includes the feature,
so viewers on Starter or Growth know what they get.

Plan tags: `ALL` = every plan · `GROWTH+` = Growth, Pro · `PRO` = Pro ·
`ADD-ON` = paid add-on / usage-billed.

---

## Track A — Customer Course (published: YouTube + course platform)

Lessons run 3–6 minutes. Every lesson follows the same structure:

1. **Hook** (0:00–0:15): what you'll be able to do by the end.
2. **Context** (≈0:30): where the feature sits and why it matters.
3. **Walkthrough** (2–4 min): real clicks in the app, with zoom-ins and highlights.
4. **Pro tip** (≈0:20): one non-obvious tip.
5. **Recap + next lesson** (≈0:15).

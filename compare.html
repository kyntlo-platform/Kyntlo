Evaluation framework

What Kyntlo Reviews Before Replacing a Fragmented Stack

A professional migration is not only about moving tools. It is about understanding which workflows must be preserved, which should be simplified, and which should be automated once the data is connected.

Lead Sources

Website forms, paid campaigns, social messages, calls, referrals, and offline sources should map into the correct pipeline.

Communication Channels

Email, SMS, chat, calls, and supported social channels need clear ownership and response expectations.

Sales Process

Pipeline stages, appointment logic, reminders, tasks, and handoffs should reflect how the team actually sells.

Reporting Needs

Decision-makers should know which sources, campaigns, appointments, and opportunities are driving progress.

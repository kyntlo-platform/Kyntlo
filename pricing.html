Which plan is right for you?

The best plan depends on how many people use the workspace, how many leads you manage, which channels you need, and how much of the work you want AI to take off your plate.

01Starter

You're getting organized. You need CRM, lead capture, booking, and a small number of users before adding deeper automation.

02Growth

You're losing leads to slow follow-up. You need multiple pipelines, an omnichannel inbox, and workflow automation to stay consistent.

03Pro

You're ready for AI everywhere. Voice AI, advanced chatbot, reviews, reactivation, call tracking, and deep reporting in one system.

04Customized

Your operation doesn't fit a box. Choose your own features, limits, and services with our team — and pay only for what you pick.

Monthly

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time — your workspace, contacts, and automations move with you.

How does the Customized plan work?

Tell our sales team which features, limits, and services you need. We scope a plan around your operation and quote a price for exactly that mix — nothing more.

What are usage costs?

Messages, call minutes and AI usage are billed on top of your plan, at the published rates above — you only pay for what you actually use. Most small teams spend $15–$60 a month here. Nothing is charged at a rate that is not printed on this page.

Is there a free trial?

Every standard plan starts with a 14-day free trial — no setup fees, cancel anytime. See our refund policy for details.

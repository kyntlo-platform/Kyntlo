# v3 ad copy — five formats, one offer

The offer is the same in all five: **we measure your reply gap, free, in 24
hours.** Nothing else is asked for. Five formats argue for it five ways, which
is what makes the test readable — if one wins, we know *why* it won.

Destination: Click-to-WhatsApp for the Dubai set, `kyntlo.ai/leak-test` for the
US set once that page exists. CTA button: **Send message** (Dubai) /
**Learn more** (US).

No testimonials, no logos, no plan prices, and no figure that is not either
from the 62-clinic audit or arithmetic the reader can check on screen.

---

## 1 — VSL  ·  `v3-vsl`  ·  S-tier  ·  cold scaler

**Primary text**

> There is a number almost no clinic measures, and it is the one that decides
> how many of your ads turn into appointments.
>
> It is not how fast your team replies. It is how long a request sits before
> anybody *could* reply — the gap between the message arriving and the clinic
> opening.
>
> At 8pm that gap is the whole night. Over a weekend it is longer. Nobody is
> being slow; the clinic is closed. But the patient isn't waiting — they
> message the next clinic on the page they found you on.
>
> Kyntlo answers inside that gap: checks your live availability, books, and
> confirms.
>
> We'll measure your gap before you decide anything. Free, 24 hours.

**Headline** — What happens between 8pm and 9am
**Description** — Free 24-hour reply-gap report

## 2 — Amateur investigation  ·  `v3-audit`  ·  A-tier  ·  cold scaler

**Primary text**

> I opened 62 Dubai clinic websites in one afternoon. All of them were running
> Instagram ads that week. I only wanted to know one thing: what the Book Now
> button actually does.
>
> 35 of 62 opened a form. Someone retypes every one of those by hand.
> 18 of 62 had no booking at all — the button was a phone number.
> 43 of 62 ran it through WhatsApp. Recorded nowhere, assigned to nobody.
> 8 of 62 had a real calendar. Not one of those eight sent a reminder or
> chased a no-show.
>
> Every one of the 62 was paying for clicks that same week.
>
> I'll check yours the same way. Free, 24 hours, and you keep the report
> whatever you decide.

**Headline** — I checked 62 Dubai clinics. Here's what I found.
**Description** — Same check on yours, free

## 3 — David & Goliath  ·  `v3-stack`  ·  A-tier  ·  cold-capable

**Primary text**

> A booking tool. A WhatsApp inbox. An email app. A spreadsheet. A reminder
> app. An invoice tool.
>
> Six subscriptions that don't talk to each other — and every gap between them
> is a lead you already paid for.
>
> The tools aren't the problem. The gaps between them are: the enquiry that
> never reached the calendar, the booking that never got a reminder, the
> no-show nobody chased.
>
> Kyntlo is one of them instead of six. One inbox, one calendar, one pipeline,
> one login.
>
> Before you move anything, we'll show you where your leads are falling
> through. Free, 24 hours.

**Headline** — Six tools, five gaps, one bill each month
**Description** — See where the leads fall through

## 4 — Objection handling  ·  `v3-objections`  ·  B-tier  ·  converter

**Primary text**

> "We already have a system." So did all 62 clinics we audited. 35 of them
> call a form a system.
>
> "My team handles the messages." Until 8pm. That's when a lot of them arrive.
>
> "Switching sounds like a nightmare." Nothing switches for 14 days. Same
> number, same channels, nothing moved.
>
> "What if it doesn't work?" You keep the report, and we go away.
>
> Free reply-gap report, 24 hours. That's the whole ask.

**Headline** — Four reasons not to, answered
**Description** — Nothing switches for 14 days

*Audience note: retargeting only — video viewers at 50%+ and site visitors.
These answers are only interesting to someone who already has the objection.*

## 5 — Educational infographic  ·  `v3-timeline`  ·  B-tier  ·  converter

**Primary text**

> This is the anatomy of a lead you already paid for.

> 19:41 — the ad works. Someone asks for an appointment.
> 19:41 — nobody is in the clinic.
> 23:00 — they message two more clinics.
> 09:12 — reception opens, and replies.
>
> 13 hours and 31 minutes after you paid for that click.
>
> The ad did its job. The thirteen hours undid it. By 09:12 the appointment
> belongs to whoever answered that evening.
>
> Kyntlo answers at 19:41. We'll measure your own gap first — free, 24 hours.

**Headline** — 13 hours 31 minutes
**Description** — The gap between the click and the reply

*Audience note: retargeting only, same pools as #4.*

## What the test is actually asking

| # | Format | Claim under test |
|---|--------|------------------|
| 1 | VSL | The buyer will sit through a mechanism explanation if it names something they've never had named |
| 2 | Investigation | Specific, checkable field data beats any claim about the product |
| 3 | David & Goliath | The villain is tool sprawl, not a competitor |
| 4 | Objections | Warm traffic stalls on four things, and all four have a one-line answer |
| 5 | Infographic | One timeline carries the whole argument without a voice |

Ads 1–3 go cold. Ads 4–5 go to retargeting, so they are not competing with
1–3 for the same impression and the read stays clean.

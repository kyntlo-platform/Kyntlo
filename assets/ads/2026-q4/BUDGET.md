# Budget — 20,000 EGP per month

Rates used: **48 EGP / USD**, **3.67 AED / USD**. EGP moves; check the live
rate before committing, and re-read this page if it has shifted much.

| Period | Budget |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly | **20,000 EGP** ≈ $417 ≈ AED 1,530 |
| Daily | **658 EGP** ≈ $13.71 ≈ AED 50 |

The campaign still targets the **UAE** — Dubai first. The creative names Dubai
businesses and quotes AED figures, and all 109 prospect records are Emirati.
Only the funding currency has changed, not the market. If the intention was to
advertise *into Egypt* instead, say so: the media plan gets far cheaper, but
every asset needs rewriting, because a UAE clinic audit persuades nobody in
Cairo.

---

## The finding that decides the whole plan

Ad platforms have minimum daily spends. At 20,000 EGP a month, most of them
are out of reach — not because the budget is small, but because it is below
their floor.

| Platform | Its daily minimum | EGP/month to meet it | Share of your budget | |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| **TikTok** | $20 per ad group | 29,184 | **146%** | Impossible — costs more than your whole budget |
| **LinkedIn** | $10 per campaign | 14,592 | 73% | Would leave 5,400 EGP for everything else |
| **YouTube** | ~$10 practical | 14,592 | 73% | Same problem |
| **Snapchat** | $5 | 7,296 | 36% | Possible, but it is the weakest channel |
| **Meta** | ~$5 workable | 7,296 | 36% | Possible, and it is the strongest channel |

**So the honest answer to "split it across all platforms" is that you cannot.**
TikTok alone would cost more than the entire month. Dividing 658 EGP a day six
ways gives each platform about 110 EGP — roughly $2.30 — which is under every
one of those floors. The ads would either be rejected at setup or would
deliver so thinly that nothing is learnable.

At this level there is one viable paid platform, and it happens to be the
right one.

Everything runs from `01-META` and `06-GOOGLE-BUSINESS`. The other four
folders stay packed until the budget reaches the ladder below.

### Why retargeting waits until week 3

It needs roughly 1,000 people in the pixel pool to work at all. At this spend
that takes about three weeks. Turning it on early just splits a small budget
across an audience that does not exist yet.

### Why the ABM line survives at 2,000 EGP

It is the cheapest line here and the only one that makes something you already
own work better. 66 EGP a day shows the 47 interior records and 62 clinic
records your ads, so the cold email lands on a name they recognise. Judge it
by **outbound reply rate**, never by its own cost per lead — and write down
today's reply rate now, or you will have nothing to compare against.

## What 20,000 EGP a month actually returns

| Stage | Per month | Basis |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Impressions | ~23,000 | CPM $12 |
| Clicks | ~230 | CTR 1.0%, CPC ~$1.20 |
| Leads | **~16** | 11 cold + 5 retargeting |
| Qualified | ~7 | 45% |
| Calls held | ~2–3 | Higher rate than the big plan — see below |
| **Signed** | **~1** | Month 2 onward |

Month one will likely produce **zero to one**, because the learning phase eats
most of it. Month two should produce one. By month three the pixel has data
and the same spend produces slightly more.

### The economics are good even though the volume is small

One customer a month at AED 1,500–4,000 monthly, against AED 1,530 of spend,
pays the month's advertising back in **about one month**. That is a healthier
ratio than the AED 44,000 plan projected. What you are giving up is speed and
certainty, not efficiency.

### The one genuine advantage of a small budget

Sixteen leads a month is a number one person can work by hand. Every single
lead can be called, chased, and personally followed up — which is why the
call-booking rate above is higher than the large-budget model assumed. At 340
leads a month that is impossible and the funnel leaks. Use it while you have
it.

## Six rules for running at this level

**1. One market, not seven emirates.** Target **Dubai only**. Spreading 658
EGP a day across the whole UAE means never reaching anyone often enough to be
remembered.

**2. One niche, and make it clinics.** The clinic assets — `ad-02-62-clinics`
and the six-frame carousel — carry only measured numbers and ship today. The
Tuesday Test still needs the Day 0 figures. Start with what is ready.

**3. Optimise for landing page views for the first three weeks**, not two.
Meta wants ~50 conversions per ad set per week; you will produce about four.
The pixel needs a cheaper, more frequent event to learn from before you ask it
to find buyers.

**4. One ad set, two ads, nothing else.** Every additional ad set divides an
already thin budget. Two ads in one CBO is the whole structure.

**5. Wait longer before judging.** The kill rules say wait until 3× target
cost per lead has been spent. At this budget that is most of a month. Judging
an ad in week one here is guessing.

**6. Reinvest before you increase.** When a customer signs, that revenue funds
the next budget step. Do not raise spend from savings — raise it from the
thing the spend produced.

## The ladder — when to add each platform

Each step is funded by the customers the step below it produced. Do not skip.

| Monthly budget | Add | Why now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **20,000 EGP** | Meta + Google Business | The only two that work at this level |
| **35,000 EGP** | **+ Snapchat** (7,300) or **YouTube** (14,600) | Snapchat is cheaper and produces the Arabic asset you need for Saudi. YouTube gives a second algorithm to confirm the register test. Pick one. |
| **50,000 EGP** | **+ LinkedIn** (14,600) | Meta is proven and stable by now. LinkedIn costs 3–4× per lead but delivers ~65% qualified against Meta's 40%, so it earns a place once you can afford both. |
| **80,000 EGP** | **+ TikTok** (29,200) | Its ad-group minimum is the highest of the five. Last in, and only once everything else is producing. |

Roughly: **one new platform per two signed customers.** That keeps the media
plan growing from revenue rather than from optimism.

## If the budget has to go further

Three options, in order of how much I would recommend them.

**Reduce the market, not the platform count.** Dubai only, and the clinic
niche only. Same money, far higher frequency against the people who matter.
This is the right answer and costs nothing.

**Use the free channels harder.** Google Business posts are free. Organic
Instagram and TikTok posting of the same creative costs nothing but time, and
if a post earns real engagement organically, promoting *that exact post* beats
a clean ad — the engagement becomes part of the creative. The outbound engine
already running is also free.

**Advertise into Egypt instead.** CPMs run a fraction of UAE rates, so 20,000
EGP buys several times the reach. But it is a different business decision, not
a budget tweak: every asset would need rewriting, the ticket sizes are lower,
and the founder's Dubai credibility does not transfer. Worth discussing
separately, not worth doing by halves.

Data Processing Agreement

Effective 6 September 2026 · Kyntlo for Artificial Intelligence Tools, Cairo, Egypt. This DPA is incorporated into the Terms of Service and governs Kyntlo's processing of personal data on behalf of the Customer. For a countersigned copy, email privacy@kyntlo.ai.

1. Definitions

  • "Data Protection Laws" means all applicable worldwide privacy regulations, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation, the UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act, and the Egypt Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151/2020).
  • "Sub-processor" means any third party engaged by Kyntlo (the Processor) to process personal data, including our platform infrastructure provider and its underlying cloud partners.
  • "Standard Contractual Clauses" means the clauses approved by the European Commission for the lawful transfer of personal data to third countries.

2. Roles and Scope of Processing

  • Controller and processor. The Customer is the data controller of all Customer Data. Kyntlo is a data processor acting strictly on the Customer's documented instructions.
  • Lawful basis warranty. The Customer represents and warrants that it has secured all notices, consents and lawful bases required to collect and transfer personal data into the Kyntlo platform.
  • Customer instructions. Kyntlo shall have no liability where the Customer's instructions or data collection practices violate applicable Data Protection Laws. If Kyntlo reasonably believes an instruction violates the GDPR or another applicable law, it may suspend the affected processing without penalty.

3. Sub-Processing and Infrastructure

  • Authorised sub-processors. The Customer grants Kyntlo general written authorisation to engage sub-processors to deliver the platform. Kyntlo's primary infrastructure is provided by a specialist platform infrastructure partner, which in turn runs on major cloud services including Google Cloud Platform and Amazon Web Services. The current named list of sub-processors, with each provider's identity, processing location and function, is provided on written request to privacy@kyntlo.ai and forms part of this Annex.
  • Objection rights. Kyntlo will notify the Customer of intended changes concerning the addition of a sub-processor. The Customer may object within 15 days; if the objection cannot be resolved, the Customer's sole remedy is to terminate the Agreement.
  • Upstream dependencies. Kyntlo is a software-as-a-service provider built on third-party cloud infrastructure. To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, Kyntlo disclaims liability for data breaches, server outages or delivery failures originating directly from an underlying sub-processor or a downstream telecommunications carrier. Where mandatory law makes a processor liable for its sub-processors, that law prevails over this paragraph.

4. Technical and Organisational Measures

  • Security standards. Kyntlo implements and maintains industry-standard technical and organisational measures, set out in Annex II, designed to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss or alteration.
  • Confidentiality. Kyntlo ensures that all personnel authorised to process personal data are bound by strict obligations of confidentiality.

5. Personal Data Breach Notification

  • Incident response. In the event of a confirmed personal data breach affecting the Customer's workspace, Kyntlo will notify the Customer without undue delay, and in no event later than 72 hours after becoming aware of the breach.
  • Remediation. Kyntlo will provide reasonable assistance to the Customer in mitigating the breach and meeting the Customer's own regulatory notification obligations.

6. Data Subject Rights

  • Assistance. Kyntlo provides technical features allowing the Customer to fulfil data subject requests, including deletion, access and portability.
  • Redirection. If Kyntlo receives a data subject request directly from the Customer's end user, Kyntlo will not respond directly but will forward the request to the Customer.

7. International Data Transfers

  • Standard Contractual Clauses. For any transfer of personal data from the European Economic Area, Switzerland or the UK to Kyntlo in Egypt or to its sub-processors, the parties agree that the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Module Two, controller to processor) apply and are incorporated by reference.
  • UK Addendum. For data governed by the UK GDPR, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum applies alongside the Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Audit Rights

  • Documentary audits. On written request, Kyntlo will make available the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, in the form of security documentation, ISO certificates or SOC 2 reports provided by our sub-processors.
  • Limitation. To preserve the security of a multi-tenant cloud environment, physical on-site audits or penetration tests of Kyntlo's servers by the Customer are not permitted.

9. Return and Deletion of Data

  • On termination. On termination or expiry of the Customer's subscription, Kyntlo shall, at the Customer's choice, delete or return all personal data.
  • Routine deletion. Where no explicit instruction is given, Kyntlo and its sub-processors routinely purge inactive workspace data within 30 to 90 days of account termination, unless retention is required by applicable law.

10. Limitation of Liability

Claims brought under this DPA are subject to the limitation of liability in the Terms of Service. Kyntlo's total aggregate liability across all privacy claims shall not exceed the subscription fees paid by the Customer in the three months preceding the incident giving rise to the liability, except where applicable mandatory law does not permit that limitation.

11. Annex I — Details of Processing

Subject matter and duration. Processing is necessary to provide the Kyntlo CRM, automation workflows, AI tools and omnichannel communication platform. It lasts for the duration of the Customer's active subscription.

Categories of data subject. The Customer's end users, prospects, leads, employees, contractors and calendar invitees.

Categories of personal data.

  • Identity and contact data — names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses.
  • Business and CRM data — lead status, company names, job titles, calendar appointments.
  • Communication data — SMS logs, call recordings, email bodies, web-chat transcripts.
  • System metadata — IP addresses, device identifiers, browser types.

Sensitive data limitations. The platform is not configured to process Payment Card Industry data directly — that is tokenised by the payment gateway — or Protected Health Information under HIPAA, unless a separate Business Associate Agreement is executed.

12. Annex II — Technical and Organisational Measures

Kyntlo, together with its sub-processors, maintains the following measures:

  • Encryption. All data transmitted across public networks is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher. Data stored in databases is encrypted at rest using AES-256.
  • Access controls. Role-based access control within the application, and mandatory multi-factor authentication for Kyntlo administrative access to production environments.
  • Physical security. Kyntlo relies on cloud data centres maintaining biometric access control, 24/7 security staffing and CCTV monitoring, certified to SOC 2 and ISO 27001.
  • Resilience and backups. Automated daily database backups distributed across multiple availability zones to ensure restoration after a physical or technical incident.
  • Anonymisation for AI processing. Personal data is stripped of identifiers when aggregated to train or optimise internal routing and workflow models. No personal data is transferred to public or generalised third-party foundation AI models.

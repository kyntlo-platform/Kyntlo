What the report checks

Six things that decide whether
a stranger calls you or a competitor.

01 — Findability

Can they even find you?

Whether your business appears in the local listings and map results people actually look at, and how prominently.

02 — Listings

Do your details agree?

Name, address, phone and opening hours across every place they are published. Contradictions quietly cost you calls.

03 — Reputation

What do the reviews say?

How many, how recent, what rating, and how many of them nobody ever replied to.

04 — Website

Does the site do anything?

Whether a visitor can book, enquire or pay without leaving — or whether it is a brochure that just sits there.

05 — Responsiveness

What happens after hours?

Whether an enquiry made outside working hours has any route to an answer before the next morning.

06 — Competitors

How do you compare?

The same measures for businesses like yours nearby, so the numbers have something to mean.

1. Overview

Kyntlo uses limited cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to operate the website, remember cookie banner preferences, support embedded tools, improve user experience, and help secure our services. Cookies are small files or identifiers stored on your browser or device.

This Cookie Policy should be read together with the Privacy Policy. For this policy, "Kyntlo," "we," "us," and "our" refers to Kyntlo for Artificial Intelligence Tools.

2. Types of Cookies and Technologies We May Use

  • Essential technologies: Required for website operation, navigation, security, page rendering, cookie banner preference storage, embedded booking tools, and basic service functionality.
  • Preference technologies: Used to remember choices such as cookie banner response, display preferences, form state, or similar settings where supported.
  • Embedded widget technologies: Tools such as the LeadConnector chat widget and booking calendar, Forminit forms, the Whop checkout, the Silktide consent manager, Google Fonts, social media links, or Kyntlo Hub login pages may use their own cookies or similar technologies when loaded or interacted with.
  • Analytics or marketing technologies: Kyntlo does not intentionally run separate advertising cookies on the marketing website unless they are added later. If added, Kyntlo will seek consent where required and update this policy.

3. Third-Party Technologies

Kyntlo may use third-party services for booking, forms, hosting, payments, security, customer support, maps, social media links, and platform login. These providers may set or read cookies and similar technologies according to their own policies.

Where required by applicable law, Kyntlo will seek consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

4. Your Choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies, receive alerts before cookies are stored, or limit certain types of tracking.

If you block all cookies, some website functions, embedded widgets, booking features, forms, or platform experiences may not work correctly.

5. Updates to This Policy

Kyntlo may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

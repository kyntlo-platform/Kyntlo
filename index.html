Offer details

Questions, answered

What exactly is discounted?

Your first quarter (three months) on the Starter, Growth or Pro package is 50% off when you choose quarterly billing. The discount covers the package subscription. Taxes are added and shown at checkout, and usage-based costs such as SMS, calls, email sending and AI messages are billed separately at wholesale rates.

Do I need a promo code?

No. Choose quarterly billing and the checkout applies the 50% launch discount to your first three months automatically, at exactly the prices shown on this page. If you switch to monthly billing, the discount is removed.

Does the 14-day free trial still apply?

Yes. You start with a 14-day free trial and nothing is charged today. Your discounted quarter is billed only when the trial ends, and you can cancel before then at no cost.

What happens after the first three months?

After the launch quarter, your package renews at its standard price plus taxes. You can upgrade, downgrade or cancel at any time, and your contacts, pipelines and automations stay with you when you switch plans.

How long is the offer available?

For a limited time only, so start your trial now to claim it. Once you check out with the offer, your price is locked in: your first three months are billed at 50% off, even if the offer ends afterwards.

Can you help me move from my current tools?

Yes. Book a live demo and our team will walk through your current setup, what moves over, and which package fits best.

What we build

You don't buy features. You buy a system that runs.

Most businesses do not need all six systems on day one. They need the one leak that is costing them most, closed properly. These are the builds we do most often — configured, trained on your business, tested end to end, and live before you are asked to run anything yourself.

The Night Shift

"You already have a day team. This is the night shift."

Everything that answers after hours, as one system. The phone is picked up, the DM is replied to, the appointment is booked inside the conversation, and you get a summary of everything that happened overnight.

Best for Home services · Clinics · Salons · Restaurants

Revenue Recovery Sprint

"Give us your old customer list and one week."

A fixed one-week campaign against the customers who have gone quiet, with a reported outcome: how many were contacted, how many replied, how many booked, and what it earned. No ad spend.

Best for Any business with 300+ past customers

The No-Show Killer

"That is not a patient problem. It is a reminder problem."

Confirmation on booking, a reminder the day before, a nudge on the morning, intake forms completed in advance, an optional deposit, and automatic rebooking outreach for anyone who misses.

Best for Dental · Medical · Med spas · Physio · Salons

Speed-to-Lead

"Every enquiry. Under sixty seconds. Measured."

Every enquiry from every channel gets a meaningful, personalised first response in under a minute — and a dashboard that proves it happened, so the promise is auditable rather than aspirational.

Best for Agencies · Real estate · Home services · High-ticket services

Reputation Engine

"Six hundred happy customers. Eleven reviews."

A closed loop: every completed job asks for a review, replies get drafted, unhappy feedback routes privately to you first, and the best reviews become social content on their own. Requests go to every customer, not only the happy ones.

Best for Every local business

Membership Machine

"Stop selling treatments. Start selling memberships."

Turns one-time buyers into recurring members: tier design, purchase flow, onboarding, community access, and a signal that flags a member who is drifting weeks before they cancel.

Best for Med spas · Gyms & studios · Coaches · Pet services

How teams use Kyntlo

The sentence we hear in every first call

Five kinds of business, five different leaks, one pattern underneath: the money is not lost on the ads. It is lost in the gap between someone reaching out and someone answering. These are the sentences owners actually say, and roughly what each one costs a month.

Home services

“I'm under a sink. The phone rings. I lose the job.”

Twenty to forty calls a month go unanswered because the person who would answer them is the person doing the work. The AI voice agent picks up, works out what they need, and books it into the diary — and if a call is still missed, a text goes back within seconds while they are deciding who to try next.

Typical leak $1,500 – $3,000 / month

Plumbing, HVAC, electrical, roofing2–15 vans
Clinics & dental

“A quarter of them just don't turn up.”

Confirmation on booking, a reminder the day before, a nudge on the morning, and intake forms filled in before arrival. No-show rates typically fall from around a quarter of the book to about one in ten.

Dental, medical, med spas1–4 locations

“I answer DMs at midnight and still have empty chairs.”

The AI answers the pricing and availability questions in about forty seconds, at any hour, and books straight into the calendar. Clients who have not been in for six weeks get a message referencing what they last had.

Overview

A complete growth cycle, not a single campaign

Phase 1A timeline

Two-week implementation
& technical setup plan

Four delivery phases across fourteen days — from DNS and channel authentication through to live attribution dashboards.

01
Days 1–3

Foundation, Integrations & Lead Ingestion

  • Domain & DNS Configuration: connect promotemylistings.com CNAME and A records to Kyntlo and verify SSL certificates for landing page hosting.
  • Channel Authentication: authenticate Facebook, Instagram and Email into Kyntlo's Omnichannel Inbox.
  • Zillow Lead Capture Setup: determine and implement workflows and data mapping to parse Zillow agent inquiries into CRM contacts automatically.
  • Workspace Provisioning: set up user access and permission profiles for Ardalan and team members.
02
Days 4–7

Landing Pages & Ad Infrastructure

  • Funnel Construction: build high-converting landing pages on Kyntlo tailored for agents seeking listing video ads.
  • Ad Manager Integration: link Meta ad accounts to enable native ad launching and ROI tracking directly from the Kyntlo command center.
  • Smart Form Redirection: configure signup forms to direct realtors immediately to the signup page.
03
Days 8–11

AI Training & Automation Workflows

  • AI Chatbot Deployment: train the chatbot knowledge base on Promote My Listing package tiers (10K, 20K, 100K view campaigns) and service parameters.
  • First-Touch Communication: build automated triggers that send an instant welcome email message upon lead capture.
04
Days 12–14

Dashboard Alignment, Testing & Launch

  • Pipeline Mapping: establish visual pipeline stages — Signed Up, No Listing, Video Created, Ad Live, Needs Follow-Up.
  • End-to-End Testing: test Zillow lead capture, AI chatbot responses and Meta ad tracking.
  • Analytics Setup & Go-Live: configure Advanced Attribution dashboards to show live metric tracking across signups, ad spend, views and converted agents.

1. Roles and Scope: Controller vs Processor

  • Kyntlo as data controller. Kyntlo is the controller for direct website visitors, marketing prospects, registered account owners, administrative contacts and billing records.
  • Kyntlo as data processor. When business clients use Kyntlo's CRM, omnichannel pipelines, voice receptionists and automated workflows to manage their own customer and lead data, the business client is the data controller and Kyntlo acts strictly as a processor executing services under contract. Our Data Processing Agreement governs that processing.
  • Customer responsibility. Customers are independently obliged to establish a lawful basis, issue appropriate privacy notices and obtain necessary consent before uploading or syncing end-user data into Kyntlo.

2. Information We Collect

  • Identity and contact data. Name, email address, phone number, company name, professional role and correspondence records.
  • Account and workspace credentials. Passwords, multi-factor authentication tokens, workspace identifiers, permission tiers and integration authorisations.
  • Customer content. Leads, pipelines, conversations, calendar appointments, campaign files and workflow records uploaded or processed by the customer.
  • Billing and transactional metadata. Subscription histories, tier selections, invoice records and tax IDs. Full payment card data is tokenised and processed directly by PCI-DSS compliant third-party gateways, and is not stored on Kyntlo servers.
  • Technical and telemetry data. IP addresses, operating system, browser configuration, referring URLs, session duration, clickstream data and server logs.

3. Legal Bases for Processing (EEA, UK and Egypt PDPL)

For individuals in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Egypt, we process personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Contractual necessity — to deliver workspace services, process transactions, authenticate accounts and provide support.
  • Legitimate interests — to safeguard network integrity, detect fraud, monitor platform security and improve workflows.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with tax reporting, statutory accounting and regulatory directives, including Egypt PDPL Law 151/2020.
  • Explicit consent — for marketing outreach, newsletters, and non-essential cookies and web trackers.

4. AI-Enabled Features and Model Training Safeguards

Kyntlo provides automated workflow, messaging and conversation assistance powered by artificial intelligence.

  • Protection of personal information. We do not use your personal information, customer CRM contacts or raw conversation logs to train generalised, public or foundation AI models. Data processed through our AI features is used exclusively to generate immediate workflow outputs for your own workspace.
  • Use of anonymised metadata. Kyntlo strips all personal identifiers from usage logs to create anonymised, aggregated metadata. We use that strictly non-personal metadata to optimise system performance, improve routing efficiency and train our own machine learning models for better automated workflows.
  • Human oversight. AI-generated outputs are predictive and advisory. Customers remain solely responsible for reviewing and verifying all AI outputs before relying on them for commercial, contractual or sensitive operational decisions.

5. Automated Decision-Making and Profiling

Kyntlo does not engage in automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects or similarly significant consequences for individuals. All automated workflows execute deterministic, customer-configured parameters.

6. Cookies, Tracking Technologies and Do Not Track

  • Cookie categories. We use strictly essential cookies necessary for navigation, security and session authentication. Analytical or marketing cookies require your prior opt-in consent through our cookie preference centre, which you can reopen at any time from the link in the footer.
  • Do Not Track and GPC. Kyntlo does not alter platform tracking practices in response to legacy "Do Not Track" browser headers, for which no common standard was agreed. Kyntlo does recognise and honour Global Privacy Control opt-out signals in supported browsers.

7. Third-Party Integrations and External Links

Kyntlo incorporates embedded third-party tools such as booking widgets, form providers, mapping services and payment processors, and our pages may link to sites we do not operate. Kyntlo exercises no ownership or control over third-party applications, websites or gateways. Kyntlo disclaims responsibility for the privacy policies, tracking mechanisms, security practices and content hosted on third-party domains. You access them at your own discretion and subject to those third parties' terms.

8. International Data Transfers

Where personal information originating from the EEA, UK, Switzerland or Egypt is transferred to countries lacking an adequacy decision, Kyntlo ensures lawful protection by executing Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, and the cross-border authorisation frameworks required by Egypt's PDPL.

9. Data Retention and Security

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was gathered, maintain business accounts, resolve disputes and comply with statutory, tax or legal retention requirements.

Kyntlo maintains administrative, organisational and technical safeguards including TLS encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, and role-based access restrictions.

10. Protection of Children

Kyntlo is designed and marketed exclusively for enterprise and commercial business use. We do not knowingly solicit, collect or process personal information from individuals under sixteen years of age. If we discover that data from a minor under 16 has been collected without verified parental or guardian consent, we will promptly purge those records.

11. European Union and United Kingdom Rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Data subjects in the EEA and UK have the right to:

  • request access to, rectification of, or erasure of their personal data;
  • request restriction of, or object to, specific processing activities;
  • request portability to another vendor in a structured, machine-readable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing;
  • lodge a complaint with an EU supervisory authority or the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

12. United States Rights (CCPA / CPRA and State Privacy Acts)

Residents of California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas and other applicable states hold specific statutory rights:

  • Right to know, access, delete and correct. Request disclosure of collected personal data, and request correction or deletion of records.
  • Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information. Kyntlo does not sell personal data for monetary consideration. To opt out of cross-context behavioural advertising or data sharing, use the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link in our footer, or signal via Global Privacy Control.
  • Authorised agent. You may designate an authorised agent registered with the relevant state authority to submit privacy requests on your behalf.

13. Australia, Canada and Egypt

Residents of Australia and Canada hold rights under the Privacy Act and PIPEDA respectively, including a 30-day response window for access and deletion requests and the right to escalate to the OAIC or the OPC.

Data subjects governed by Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151/2020) hold the statutory right to withdraw consent, inspect collected records, rectify erroneous files, and file grievances before the Personal Data Protection Centre.

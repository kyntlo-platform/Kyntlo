For this policy, "Kyntlo," "we," "us," and "our" refers to Kyntlo for Artificial Intelligence Tools, located at Building Lotus2 North90, After Chill Out Lotus, Cairo Governorate 11234, Egypt.

2. How We Collect Personal Information

Kyntlo may collect personal information through direct and indirect methods depending on how you use the website, forms, support channels, embedded tools, and platform services.

Direct methods: information you provide through website forms, free trial requests, demo bookings, support tickets, email, phone calls, payment flows, onboarding documents, platform account setup, and other communications with Kyntlo.

Indirect methods: information generated when you use the website or platform, information provided by customer administrators or authorized team members, information from trusted service providers and integrations used to deliver the service, public business sources, and third-party tools such as booking, chat, forms, payment, email, messaging, or analytics providers where enabled.

Kyntlo does not intentionally collect information in a way designed to identify individuals unless the information is needed for a legitimate website, service, support, billing, security, or customer workflow purpose.

3. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Kyntlo, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Identity and contact information: name, email address, phone number, company name, role, location, and messages you send to us.
  • Account information: login details, user roles, workspace settings, plan selection, billing status, support requests, and service preferences.
  • Customer content: contacts, conversations, forms, surveys, appointment details, pipeline records, workflow configurations, campaign data, and files uploaded or created in the platform.
  • Billing information: subscription records, invoices, usage charges, payment status, and payment metadata. Full card details are handled by payment processors and are not intended to be stored by Kyntlo.
  • Usage and device information: IP address, browser type, pages viewed, referring URLs, device identifiers, approximate location, logs, diagnostic data, and cookie or similar tracking data.
  • Demo and support information: calendar booking details, call notes, troubleshooting information, and communications with sales or support.

  • Provide, configure, secure, maintain, and improve the Kyntlo website and platform.

  • Create and manage accounts, subscriptions, permissions, billing, usage records, and service notices.

Where privacy law requires a legal basis, we rely on contract performance, legitimate interests, consent, and legal obligations depending on the purpose and the context of processing.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Kyntlo marketing website currently uses limited essential browser storage, including storage used to remember cookie banner preferences. The website may also load embedded third-party tools such as chat widgets, Forminit forms, Calendly booking, payment links, maps, or platform login links, and those providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies when loaded or interacted with.

Kyntlo does not intentionally run separate advertising cookies on the website unless they are added later. If non-essential analytics, marketing, or third-party tracking technologies are introduced, Kyntlo will seek consent where required by applicable law and update the Cookie Policy accordingly.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect embedded forms, booking tools, chat, payment pages, or platform functionality.

8. Retention and Security

Kyntlo generally retains website inquiry, demo, free trial, support, and account-related personal information for up to 12 months after the relevant request, customer relationship, or account activity ends, unless a longer period is required for legal, tax, accounting, dispute, fraud prevention, security, contractual, or customer-configured platform reasons.

Customer workspace data may follow the customer's account configuration, subscription status, deletion request, export requirements, and applicable legal obligations. Customers should maintain their own retention practices for contacts, campaigns, conversations, and uploaded data they control inside the platform.

Kyntlo uses administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, and disclosure. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so customers should also maintain strong passwords, access controls, and internal data handling practices.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and the law that applies to your request, you may have several rights in relation to personal information Kyntlo holds about you, including:

  • Right to be informed: to know how we collect, use, share, retain, and protect personal information as explained in this policy.
  • Right of access and copy: to request access to personal information we hold about you and obtain a copy where applicable.
  • Right to rectification: to request correction of inaccurate, incorrect, incomplete, or outdated personal information.
  • Right to deletion or destruction: to request deletion of personal information in certain circumstances, unless retention is required by law, contract, security, accounting, dispute, or legitimate business requirements.
  • Right to withdraw consent: to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, without affecting processing that occurred before withdrawal.
  • Right to restriction, objection, or portability: to restrict processing, object to certain processing, or request portability where applicable law provides those rights.
  • Right to complain: to contact a privacy regulator or supervisory authority where applicable.

If your information is held by a Kyntlo customer inside that customer's workspace, please contact that customer first. Kyntlo may need to support the customer in responding to your request.

Platform

What is Kyntlo?

Kyntlo is an AI-powered growth operations platform for CRM, lead capture, conversations, booking, funnels, workflow automation, reviews, reporting, and customer follow-up. It is designed to reduce the number of separate tools a business needs to run sales and customer communication.

Who is Kyntlo built for?

Kyntlo is built for service businesses, agencies, multi-brand teams, consultants, clinics, education providers, real estate teams, local operators, and growth teams that need a more connected way to manage leads, appointments, follow-up, and customer journeys.

Can Kyntlo replace tools I already use?

In many cases, yes. Kyntlo can help replace or consolidate tools for CRM, landing pages, forms, surveys, booking calendars, unified inbox, campaigns, automations, review requests, basic websites, and reporting. Exact replacement depends on your current stack, integrations, plan, and workflow requirements.

Do I need to be technical to use Kyntlo?

No. Kyntlo is designed for operational teams, not only developers. Some advanced workflows, integrations, telecom setup, domains, AI behavior, and data migration may still require configuration, which can be reviewed during onboarding or a demo session.

Plans and Billing

What is included in the Kyntlo plans?

Kyntlo plans are structured by business stage and usage needs. Starter focuses on CRM, booking, forms, pages, and basic lead capture. Growth adds stronger automation and conversation handling. Pro unlocks advanced AI, reviews, reactivation, memberships, reporting, and call tracking. The Customized plan is built with our sales team around your exact needs: you choose which features you want, set your own limits, and pay only for that mix — ideal for larger teams, multi-brand access, and higher-volume operations.

Are taxes included in the package prices?

Yes. The package prices shown on the website include taxes, and each package card is labelled accordingly. Usage-based services, add-ons, telecom, or third-party costs may still be reviewed separately when applicable to your selected workflow.

How do I pay for a package?

Package buttons on the website open the approved EasyKash payment link. After payment, the Kyntlo team can confirm the selected plan, account information, onboarding requirements, and any setup details needed to activate the platform correctly.

Can I upgrade or change my package later?

Yes. As your team grows, you can request a plan review and move to a package that better matches your users, brands, automation needs, AI requirements, and communication volume. Any billing change should be confirmed with the Kyntlo team before it takes effect.

Are there hidden fees?

Kyntlo aims to keep plan pricing clear. Your package covers the plan capabilities listed on the pricing page. Usage-based services such as telecom, messaging, email infrastructure, premium AI, integrations, or selected add-ons may depend on actual use or activated services, so they should be reviewed before launch.

Onboarding

Can I request a demo before choosing a package?

Yes. Use the demo calendar link in the footer to book a time with Kyntlo. The team can review your workflow and recommend the best plan before you purchase.

What happens after I purchase?

After payment, Kyntlo can confirm your business details, plan, users, required channels, domains, calendar setup, pipeline structure, forms, automations, and any migration notes. The goal is to activate the account with the right operational structure from day one.

Can Kyntlo help migrate from other tools?

Kyntlo can help plan migration from common CRM, funnel, booking, inbox, and automation tools. The migration scope depends on export access, data quality, field mapping, integrations, and whether custom assets need to be rebuilt.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes, custom domains can be connected for eligible website, funnel, landing page, and tracking workflows. Domain access, DNS configuration, and verification may be required during setup.

Security and Data

Where is my data stored?

Kyntlo uses a combination of its platform environment and connected service providers to deliver CRM, messaging, automation, calendar, AI, billing, and support workflows. Customers with European or regulated data requirements should request data hosting, processor, and transfer details before launch so the correct compliance review can be completed.

Is Kyntlo suitable for European customers?

Kyntlo can support professional privacy and data-handling reviews for European customers. Before onboarding, customers should confirm controller/processor roles, data categories, approved subprocessors, retention requirements, transfer safeguards, and any Data Processing Agreement requirements.

How does Kyntlo approach security?

Kyntlo focuses on account access controls, responsible configuration, secure data handling, customer permission management, platform provider safeguards, and clear customer responsibilities. More detail is available on the Security page.

Can I request security or privacy documentation?

Yes. For privacy, data processing, security, or due-diligence requests, contact contact@kyntlo.ai or review the public Security and Privacy Policy pages.

Support

How do I contact Kyntlo?
  • Primary inquiries: contact@kyntlo.ai
  • Sales and demos: Sales@Kyntlo.ai
  • Platform support: Support@Kyntlo.ai
Where do existing customers log in?

Existing customers can access the platform through the Kyntlo Hub at https://hub.kyntlo.ai/. If you cannot access your account, contact Platform Support.

What should I include in a support request?

Include your company name, account email, affected page or workflow, screenshots if available, urgency, browser/device information, and a short description of what you expected versus what happened.

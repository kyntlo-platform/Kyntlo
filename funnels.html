No. Kyntlo builds full websites, blogs-style pages, and funnels from the same editor — with custom domains, SSL, and hosting included.

Yes. Rebuild quickly from templates, or talk to our team about assisted migration as part of onboarding.

Unlimited funnels and pages on every plan. Usage costs apply only to communication and AI resources, at the rates published on our pricing page.

Kyntlo checkouts connect to leading payment providers, including Stripe, PayPal, Square, Razorpay, and Authorize.net.

Content AI writes headlines and page copy, conversation AI answers leads generated by your funnels, and workflow AI powers the follow-up behind every step.

Natively. Every opt-in, purchase, and booking creates or updates a contact, moves pipeline stages, and can trigger automations — no Zapier required.
